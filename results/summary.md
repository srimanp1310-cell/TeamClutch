- fp16 ridge: **128.7 FLOP/byte**
- bf16 ridge: **132.7 FLOP/byte**

_Measured on the card with a 4096x4096 matmul and a 512 MB device-to-device copy, not taken from the spec sheet: this is a low-TGP part and the real figures are roughly half the published ones._

**The ridge point moves right as precision drops.** Reduced precision raises the compute roof and leaves the bandwidth roof where it is, so a workload that was compute-bound in fp32 can become *memory-bound* in bf16 — the same kernel does not simply move up, it can cross to the other side of the ridge and change which optimization comes next.
